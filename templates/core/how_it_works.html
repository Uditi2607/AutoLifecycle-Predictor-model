Methodology

How AutoLifecycle Works

A transparent look at how we analyse your vehicle and produce explainable recommendations.

The Process

Four-Stage Analysis Pipeline

1
Data Input

You register your vehicle's key attributes: age, mileage, fuel type, service history and financial details.

2
ML Analysis

Six independent ML modules analyse resale value, depreciation, health, maintenance risk and cost of ownership.

3
Decision Engine

A multi-signal decision engine weighs all module outputs to produce KEEP, MONITOR, SELL or EXCHANGE with a confidence score.

4
Explainable Report

You receive a detailed report with the reasoning behind each recommendation, charts and an estimated exchange window.

ML Engine

The Six Analysis Modules

Each module is independently implemented and can be extended or retrained without affecting the others.

1. Resale Price Predictor

A Gradient Boosting Regressor trained on verified vehicle transaction data. Features include vehicle age, mileage, fuel type, transmission and owner count.

Awaiting Training Dataset
2. Depreciation Forecaster

Projects a 10-year depreciation curve using declining balance modelling, calibrated for Indian market conditions and fuel type.

Rule-Based — Active
3. Vehicle Health Scorer

Composite 0–100 health score from five weighted components: age, mileage, service regularity, repair burden and service recency.

Rule-Based — Active
4. Maintenance Risk Assessor

Risk-level classification (LOW / MEDIUM / HIGH / CRITICAL) with an estimated annual maintenance cost and prioritised action list.

Rule-Based — Active
5. Ownership Cost Estimator

Estimates the financial cost of continued ownership vs. the opportunity cost of exchanging now, based on depreciation and maintenance projections.

Rule-Based — Active
6. KEEP / MONITOR / EXCHANGE Engine

Multi-signal decision function combining all five modules into one weighted recommendation with a confidence score and reasoning bullets.

Rule-Based — Active
Training Dataset

The resale prediction model will be trained on the Mendeley Vehicle Sales Dataset (DOI: 10.17632/8d38h82fyt.2). This dataset contains verified used-vehicle transaction records with features including year, mileage, fuel type, seller type, transmission and selling price.

Transparency Commitments
  • All rule-based estimates are clearly labelled as prototypes.
  • ML models are only marked as "trained" after verified dataset ingestion and evaluation.
  • Every recommendation includes a detailed reason list.
  • Model training and prediction are strictly separated.
  • All model artifacts are versioned and stored in artifacts/.
